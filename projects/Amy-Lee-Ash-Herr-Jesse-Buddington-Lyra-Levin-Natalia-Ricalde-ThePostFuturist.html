The mob is fickle. Win admiration or perish. #NvidiaYolo42069 #DumbMoneyVisionPro

Your attention is your power. Put yourself on the market and compete to acquire attention from the audience.

In "Pump It Up", we explore the struggle of "new money" (Red) up-and-coming companies versus "old money" (White) entrenched organizations. Both the "new" and "old" battle against each other to maintain or acquire more power.

Battle against your competitor and compete for attention. Increase your own stock price by convincing the mob to raise their arms for you.

Can you increase your own equity and dethrone your opponent?
