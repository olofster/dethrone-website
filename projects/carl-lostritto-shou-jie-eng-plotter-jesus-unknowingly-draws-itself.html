Plotter Jesus Unknowingly Draws Itself

<< Master Slave : Providence RI.

"Plotter Jesus Unknowingly Draws Itself" is a performance by the pen plotter, a machine whose job is typically only to depict what we order it to. During the performance, the plotter is elevated to depicting itself. It plots its own image, then is covered by the image, then plots itself again iteratively, being both the creator and the created.
